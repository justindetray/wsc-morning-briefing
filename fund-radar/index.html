Iran Framework Status -- Sun 5/31 AM (Trump Tougher Counter Sat + Hormuz Reassertion = Lateral Motion Not Closing)

GeopoliticalTrump Returned Tougher Revised Iran Framework Saturday -- New Delay Vector
Sources: NYT, Anadolu/Axios, Arab News, Business Times, DW

Per NYT, Anadolu/Axios, Arab News, Business Times and DW: Trump returned a revised, tougher framework back to Iran on Saturday for review. The changes strengthen nuclear-material handling provisions; Trump is reportedly "concerned about unfreezing funds" -- a deliberate echo of his long-running Obama-era JCPOA critique. The revised package is intended to pressure Iran to accept faster, but US officials concede that reaching supreme leader Mojtaba Khamenei is "difficult" and the harder terms "could mean additional delays." This is the second consecutive weekend where the framework has moved laterally rather than forward -- after Friday's two-hour Sit Room ended with no decision, Saturday's tougher counterproposal is a new delay vector rather than a closing move.
