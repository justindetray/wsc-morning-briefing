Iran-MOU Sun 6/14 Standoff Trade Map

Trump posted on Truth Social Saturday that the deal "is scheduled to get signed tomorrow" (Sun 6/14) and that "immediately after it is signed, the Hormuz Strait is OPEN TO ALL."   • Money: Iran proposed releasing half of frozen assets early; US rejected. Qatar proposed $12B package ($6B humanitarian + $6B credit line). Iran's $300B reparations demand persists.
  • Iranian framing: Javan: negotiation is "a means of managing the enemy." Khorasan: MoU is a tactical pause to prepare for "final battle."

ISW risk assessment: "An open strait under Iranian management is not a return to the pre-war status quo and would mean that Iran has accomplished a key war aim."
