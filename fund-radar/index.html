Lebanon / HezbollahIDF Seized Beaufort Castle Sun -- Hezbollah Strikes 10mi+ Deep into Israel (1st Since Apr 16 Ceasefire)
Source: Institute for the Study of War

Per ISW: IDF 36th Armored Division launched new ground offensives on both sides of the Litani River Saturday to expand the "Yellow Line"; seized Beaufort Castle Sunday 5/31 as part of the new ground offensives. Beaufort Castle is operationally significant high ground for ground-force movement observation and symbolically significant from the 1982 Israeli invasion of Lebanon. Hezbollah claimed for the first time since the April 16 ceasefire that it fired rockets >10 miles south of the Israel-Lebanon border -- including Karmiel, the Krayot suburbs of Haifa Sat-Sun; an Israeli military correspondent reported Hezbollah strikes on Acre. Hezbollah has increasingly used direct fire in protracted ground engagements since May 19. Adds a meaningful new escalation vector to Iran-framework calculus.

Iran InternalUnverified -- Pezeshkian Reportedly Submitted Resignation to Supreme Leader Mojtaba Khamenei
Source: ISW (citing anti-regime media); Iranian state media denies

Per ISW: anti-regime media citing an unspecified informed source claimed on May 31 that President Masoud Pezeshkian has submitted a resignation letter to Supreme Leader Mojtaba Khamenei, with Pezeshkian reportedly warning in an "unprecedented and critical tone" that the IRGC is wielding an outsized role in the regime. ISW-CTP cannot independently verify this report. Iranian state media has denied the report and no Iranian officials have confirmed at the time of this writing. If true, would represent a meaningful executive-IRGC rift inside the Iranian regime that could complicate any deal acceptance. Trade as risk factor pending verification.

Kurdistan / IraqIran Fired Ballistic Missile at PAK Base in Iraqi Kurdistan May 31
Source: ISW

Per ISW: IRGC fired a ballistic missile targeting a Kurdistan Freedom Party (PAK) base in Iraqi Kurdistan May 31 -- struck PAK headquarters in Darashakran, Erbil Province; no casualties reported. PAK spokesperson said Iran also struck the same base May 25 with at least 4 missiles plus drones, wounding 9. PAK claims Iranian forces and Iranian-backed Iraqi militias have attacked PAK forces over 50 times since war began Feb 28. Likely response to Kurdish opposition attack on Iranian border guard unit in Chaldran County, West Azerbaijan Province May 29.
