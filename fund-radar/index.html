US-Iran Peace Deal Confirmed -- Signing Geneva Fri 6/19

Pakistani Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif posted on X Sunday evening: "After extensive discussions, we are delighted to reveal that a Peace Deal between the United States of America and the Islamic Republic of Iran has been ACHIEVED." Iran's Supreme National Security Council and Deputy Foreign Minister Kazem Gharibabadi announced agreement on a final draft of the MoU after 15 hours of meetings with Qatari officials in Tehran.

Trump on Truth Social: "The arrangement with the Islamic Republic of Iran is now finalized. I hereby fully authorize the toll-free reopening of the Strait of Hormuz and simultaneously permit the immediate cessation of the United States Naval blockade." Vice President Vance on Fox News: deal is "a tremendous benefit for the American populace," framing it as the path to lower gasoline prices.

Caveat per ISW June 14 Special Report: The Iranian SNSC and Gharibabadi confirmed ceasefire and US blockade lift but did NOT comment on whether Iran would re-open the strait without tolls. Iran has repeatedly emphasized that the strait is under Iranian control and part of its broader deterrence strategy. ISW assesses Iran will likely retain some management functions over the strait. This is the residual risk for the energy trade post-signing.

Deal mechanics (per Pakistani and regional officials): 14-page MoU establishes 60-day post-signing framework for technical discussions on nuclear program, frozen assets, and termination of all sanctions on Iran. US sanctions on Iranian oil exports to be lifted. Strait reopens within 30 days. Deal does NOT solve thorniest issues (Iran's nuclear program, frozen assets) but offers framework for talks during the 60-day window. US and Israel appear to have fallen short of original goals of destroying Iran's missile and nuclear programs and ending support for proxies.

Beirut wildcard: Israel bombed Beirut suburbs Sunday in retaliation for Hezbollah drone incursions into northern Israel. Iranian negotiator Ghalibaf said the strike could derail the deal. Three Iranian officials said Iran called off a planned attack on Israel after Trump urged restraint. Hegseth on Face the Nation: Israel's response was "very measured," deal proceeds "not a matter of if, but when."
