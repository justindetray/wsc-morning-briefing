Universe Snapshot -- Sat 5/30 Weekend Edition (Fri 4-Peat ATH Cash Close + SPX 9th Consecutive Weekly Gain + Chicago PMI Blowout + Iran Framework Stalled)

The Widespread Capital fund universe enters the weekend with three reinforcing tailwinds and one geopolitical overhang. Friday's cash close delivered a 4-peat all-time-high session: SPX 7,580.06 (+0.22% 4th consecutive ATH), NDX 30,333.18 (+0.36% fresh ATH), DJIA 51,032.46 (+0.72% fresh ATH), RUT 2,919.34 (-0.59% pulled back from prior session), VIX 15.32 (-2.67% multi-week low) per Yahoo Finance. This marks the SPX's 9th consecutive weekly gain -- the longest weekly winning streak since 2023 per market reports. The macroeconomic backdrop got dramatically firmer Friday morning: Chicago PMI May 62.7 BLOWOUT vs 50.3 consensus (prior 49.2) per Crypto Briefing and GuruFocus -- a 13.5-point upside surprise that, combined with Thursday's Durables +7.9% MoM, paints an unambiguous goods-side re-acceleration signature. The geopolitical setup deteriorated meaningfully Friday afternoon and into Saturday morning. Per The Independent, Trump held a 2-hour Situation Room review of the draft Iran memorandum of understanding Friday afternoon and ended the meeting without a final decision. Per NYT, Trump "puts off final determination" -- unresolved provisions include the scope of Iran's missile program, disposition of its near-bomb-grade uranium plus ~10 tons of lower-enriched fuel, and the status of frozen assets. Saturday morning, Iran's foreign ministry spokesman Esmaeil Baghaei publicly stated to Al Jazeera that "no agreement has yet been completed between Tehran and Washington" -- the first explicit Iranian pushback against the "almost there" narrative that has defined this week's headlines. The Strait of Hormuz remains closed; the military blockade is still in place. This is now the 5th Iran framework to stall at the eleventh hour, validating the Sycamore warning from Run #72. Oil reacted appropriately: WTI Fri close $87.36 (+0.29%), Brent $92.05 (+1.30%) -- Brent jumped on the stall as the Hormuz risk premium re-priced higher. Polygon X:BTCUSD Fri daily close $73,368.99 (-0.23% from Thu $73,537.09) with intra-session open $73,512.55, high $74,380, low $72,362.80, volume 14,973 BTC; Saturday AM BTC=USD prints $73,538 (+0.23%) -- a modest weekend recovery as flows reset into Sunday futures reopen. Adding to the macro mosaic: Canada slipped into technical recession Friday with Q1 real GDP slightly negative -- the second consecutive negative quarter, 3 of the last 4 quarters now negative per CTV News. This is the first major-economy recession of the current cycle and a notable divergence from the persistent US strength signature.
