CLIENT FRAME
July CPI lands at 8:30 this morning into the tightest possible setup. A live CME pull (cohort 8/11) now has the September FOMC at hold 50.4% vs hike 49.6% — a genuine coin flip, eased back from the 53.4% hike lean we reported yesterday. CNN independently described the market as roughly 50/50. So this print does not merely inform the September decision; it plausibly decides it. Consensus is headline +0.1% MoM / +3.4% YoY and core +0.2% / +2.5%, with core point estimates clustering +0.20-0.22% and a right tail from Wells Fargo (+0.24%) and Deutsche Bank (+0.26%). Worth remembering that June headline actually fell 0.4% MoM, so the bar for a benign-looking rebound is low. The asymmetry is what matters: with September balanced on a knife edge and Cleveland's Hammack saying Monday that 'some number' of additional hikes may be needed and that a single 25bp move 'probably doesn't do a whole lot,' a hot core has far more room to reprice the front end than a soft one has to soothe it. Then $42B of 10-year notes prices at 1:00 pm, hours after the number. Against that, the AI complex just delivered. SMCI, CoreWeave, and Lumentum all beat and guided higher, and futures are firmer because of it — NQ +0.65%. But oil keeps grinding: Brent settled $88.91 and trades near $89.70 after a US Navy helicopter disabled a containership trying to run the blockade of Iranian ports. Equities fell about a third of a percent Tuesday while VIX also fell, which is not fear — it is a market that has hedged and is waiting.
