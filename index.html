CLIENT FRAME
July CPI landed exactly on consensus on all four lines — headline +0.1% MoM / +3.4% YoY, core +0.2% / +2.5% — and that non-event was the bullish outcome. September hike odds fell from a coin flip to hold 59.4% / hike 40.6% on the live CME tape, VIX collapsed 4.8% to 14.55, and the AI complex went vertical: CoreWeave +19.3%, Super Micro +19.0%, Lumentum +13.6%, CAVA +14.2%. Two cautions against reading this as all-clear. First, July CPI was collected before the Hormuz oil spike — gasoline actually fell 2.9% on the month, so the energy shock now in Brent near $89 simply is not in this data yet. It shows up in August and September prints, which is precisely the window the Fed is deciding in. Second, the $42B 10-year auction hours later cleared at 4.683%, the highest since 2007, with bid-to-cover slipping to 2.53x from 2.59x. Equities got the relief; the long end did not. Today's PPI at 8:30 matters more than usual because it feeds core PCE, and Hammack — the most hawkish voice on the committee — speaks fifteen minutes before it. No index made a new high: the S&P closed 0.12% under its August 7 record and the Nasdaq 100 remains 2.99% below its June peak.
